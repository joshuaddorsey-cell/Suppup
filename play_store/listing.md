## Full description

Good walks. Happy dogs.

Suppup helps dog owners discover nearby places for their next outing. Use your
current location to explore mapped walking paths, dog parks, pet shops, and
dog-friendly treat stops around you.

With Suppup you can:

- Find leash-friendly walking paths near your location
- Discover mapped dog parks and useful park details
- Locate nearby pet shops and dog-friendly treat stops
- Compare distance and view results on an interactive map
- Save favorite places on your phone
- Add personal ratings that remain private on your device
- Open walking directions in your preferred maps app

Suppup uses OpenStreetMap data and clearly labels preview examples whenever
live location or map results are unavailable. Always follow posted park rules,
local leash laws, and on-site safety guidance.

Map and place data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

## Data Safety worksheet

Suppup transmits location only for app functionality. It does not use accounts,
advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated backend.

- Approximate location: collected and shared for app functionality; optional;
  encrypted in transit; not retained by the Suppup developer.
- Precise location: collected and shared for app functionality; optional;
  encrypted in transit; not retained by the Suppup developer.
- Favorites and personal ratings: stored locally on the device; not collected
  by or shared with the Suppup developer.
- User data deletion: users can clear app storage or uninstall Suppup to remove
  locally stored favorites and ratings.

Public map and search operators may retain ordinary service logs under their own
policies. Review the final Play Console wording against the current Data Safety
definitions before submission.
