SUPPUP

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Suppup does not require an account, show ads, use analytics, or sell personal information. Location is used only to find dog-friendly places near the user.

Information Suppup uses

With permission, Suppup accesses the device's foreground location while the app is in use. Suppup uses latitude and longitude to center the map and request nearby walking paths, dog parks, pet shops, and dog-friendly treat stops. Suppup does not request background location.

How location is processed

Nearby searches send the selected coordinates over encrypted HTTPS connections to public OpenStreetMap-compatible Overpass services. Loading the map also sends the viewed map area and ordinary network information, such as an IP address, to OpenStreetMap service operators. These third-party operators may process technical logs under their own policies. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

Information stored on the device

Saved favorites and personal spot ratings remain on the device. The Suppup developer does not receive them. They can be removed by clearing Suppup's app data or uninstalling the app.

External map and directions apps

When a user chooses walking directions, Suppup opens a maps application or website installed on the device. Use of that external service is governed by its own privacy policy.

Choices and controls

Location permission is optional and can be denied or revoked in Android settings. Without permission, Suppup displays clearly labeled preview examples instead of live nearby results.

Children's privacy

Suppup is a general-audience app for dog owners and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when Suppup's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the Suppup support page.
